A new reality

From doubt
to confidence.

Across India, care leavers are turning lived experience into leadership. Through fellowships, networks and community-led initiatives, young people are creating opportunities and shaping how aftercare is understood.

This festival brings care leavers, practitioners and partners together to celebrate those achievements—and to keep building systems where every young person can thrive.

01

Rights-based

Moving beyond charity to dignity, opportunity and equal rights.

02

Strength-driven

Connecting lived experience, skills and leadership with opportunity.

03

Systemic change

Working together to improve aftercare, policy and workplaces.

04

Evidence-led

Building lasting solutions informed by lived experience.
