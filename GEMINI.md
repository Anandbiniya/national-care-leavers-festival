# Project Rules: National Care Leavers Festival

## 🌿 Brand Identity & Design System Protection
1. **Color Palette is Locked**:
   - Primary Green: `#347A3D` (`--green`)
   - Dark Green (Main text / Headings): `#245F30` (`--dark-green`, `--body-text`)
   - Deep Forest Green: `#174422` (`--deep-green`)
   - Secondary Green: `#426448` (`--secondary`)
   - Border Green: `#DDE8DC` (`--border`)
   - Accent Warm Orange: `#FF8A45` (`--orange`)
   - Accent Orange Text: `#A5481F` (`--orange-text`)
   - Soft Canvas: `#FFFCF8` (`--warm`)
   - Light Orange Tint: `#FFF0E5` (`--light-orange`)
   - Never replace or override these green tones with other arbitrary themes.

2. **Brand & Typography Integrity**:
   - Official Name: **National Care Leavers Festival** (NCLF)
   - Official Tagline: **Lived experience. Leading the future.**
   - Headings Font: `Manrope`, sans-serif
   - Body Font: `DM Sans`, sans-serif
   - Keep official assets, logos, videos, and social/contact channels intact.
